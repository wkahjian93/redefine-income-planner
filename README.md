# REDEFINE Income Planner

Phone app for recruitment conversations. Drag cases and case size to see illustrative income for Agent, UM and GM.

- `docs/` is the live app, served by GitHub Pages. `docs/calc.js` holds the rates and maths.
- `node test.js` checks the maths against the commission sheet and the company chart.
- `node serve.js` previews it on port 5191.

Override rates follow the company AAC contract. Personal rates follow the REDEFINE commission sheet.
